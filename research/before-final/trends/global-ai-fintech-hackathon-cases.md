# Global AI & FinTech hackathon cases — what actually wins

**Source:** `Global_AI_Fintech_Hackathons_Real_Cases.docx` (team document, written in Russian).
English explanation below; the **original Russian text is preserved verbatim at the end of this
file** so nothing is lost in translation.

**Why this matters to us:** the cases are FinTech, our track is Computer Vision — but the
*winning pattern* is track-independent and maps directly onto the WIUT judging criteria
(Innovation 25 / Technical 25 / Impact 25 / Presentation 15 / Business 10).
See [[research-cv-landscape-sep-2026]].

## 1. DTCC AI-Powered Hackathon — 2025
250+ participants, 35 teams, 46 organisations. DTCC committed **$250,000+ in grants** for
continued open-source development of winning solutions through FINOS.

| Place / team | Problem → solution | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold — CIBC / Reg Central** | Staff manually scan many sources of regulatory updates: slow, and late compliance is costly → AI centralises regulatory updates, identifies what changed, simplifies compliance | **$100,000** |
| **Silver — Cyber ThirdEye** | Cyber risk across many third-party vendors is hard to manage → one dashboard with per-vendor risk scores and risk visualisation | **$75,000** |
| **Bronze — Xypher / Deutsche Bank** | Manual work and errors in trade settlement; settlement-failure risk; liquidity management → AI validates trades, detects errors, optimises and forecasts liquidity, uses an AI agent to verify event chains | **$40,000** |
| **Academic — Cipher Sentinel / Duke** | Suspicious digital-wallet activity is hard to catch in real time → AI monitors wallets and flags suspicious activity | **$40,000** |

## 2. Quadcode HackAIthon — 2024
330+ applications from 9 countries, 100+ participants, 24 teams, **€40,000** total pool.

| Place / team | Problem → solution | Prize |
|---|---|---|
| **Grand Prize — TradePal** | Traders struggle to analyse charts and market conditions quickly → AI assistant gives real-time trading advice from user behaviour + market conditions; public description also mentions a **browser extension and computer vision to recognise candlestick patterns** | **€30,000** |
| **2nd — FinBot** | Trading/investing processes are complex → AI chatbot with personalised advice and real-time data analytics | €7,500 |
| **3rd — Kuzma** | Users struggle to plan budget, investments and purchases → AI personal-finance tool with predictive analytics | €2,500 |

> Note for us: even in a FinTech contest, the **grand prize used computer vision** (reading
> candlestick charts). CV as the engine inside someone else's domain problem is a winning shape.

## 3. Ripple CBDC Innovate — 2023
Online global hackathon, 1,186 participants. Phase I pool **$200,000**; 6 finalists received
$10,000 each plus a Phase II invitation with a separate **$140,000** pool.

- **ExtoPay** — last-mile digital payments and CBDC access for users with varied devices or no
  constant connectivity → CBDC solution with **offline capability**, programmable transactions,
  managed anonymity, multiple form factors.
- **Universal Parametric Climate Insurance** — climate insurance is out of reach for much of the
  population, especially in the Global South → dApp for parametric climate insurance using CBDC,
  where data and conditions automate the insurance process.

## 4. FAB x Mastercard AI Startup Challenge — 2025
200+ applications, 8 finalists. Themes: cybersecurity, generative AI in banking and finance,
customer lifecycle management, productivity, emerging fintech, ESG.
**Winner: Teammates.ai — $150,000** plus access to Mastercard's global resources and fast-tracked
entry to Start Path.
*The document is explicit that public sources don't give enough detail to reconstruct the exact
problem-solution case without guessing — good discipline, we should keep that habit.*

## 5. FAB Forward Fintech Challenge — 2025
**Winner: Inovat.** Problem: travellers find it hard to claim VAT refunds after shopping abroad →
a fully digital VAT-refund experience. Prize: **$100,000** plus a proof of concept with the bank
and access to banking/investment contacts.

## 6. What the strong solutions have in common
*(This is the core of the document — and it is directly transferable to our CV track.)*

1. **A specific, expensive problem of a real company** — not an abstract "AI chatbot".
2. **AI sits inside the process**: detection, compliance, settlement, risk scoring, forecasting,
   automation. It is the mechanism, not a decoration.
3. **A clear user and a clear buyer**: a bank, a compliance team, a trader, a CISO, a financial
   institution. Someone whose budget it comes out of.
4. **The result is measurable**: less manual work, fewer errors, faster processing, better risk
   detection.
5. **A working prototype/demo is showable in 24-48 hours**, even though the production system
   would be far more complex.

## 7. Directions the document suggests (FinTech track)
AI agents + finance · fraud detection / AML · AI regulatory compliance · AI document processing ·
trade settlement automation · third-party cyber risk · credit risk / credit scoring ·
cross-border payments · personal finance AI · climate / parametric insurance + finance.

## 8. How we apply this to Computer Vision at WIUT
| Pattern from the cases | Our version |
|---|---|
| Specific expensive problem of a named organisation | Name the buyer in the pitch: a bank's onboarding desk, the National TB Program, a water authority, a road agency |
| AI inside the process | Our model must *replace a manual step* (re-typing documents, reading X-rays, counting livestock), not just display boxes |
| Clear user + buyer | Say who pays and out of which budget line |
| Measurable result | Bring numbers: X% less manual work, Y seconds vs Z minutes, baseline vs us |
| Demo in 24-48h | Freeze scope early; a narrow working demo beats a broad broken one |
| Honest about unknowns | The document refuses to invent details about Teammates.ai — we mark `(unverified)` the same way |

**Sharpest single takeaway:** winners don't pitch a model, they pitch *a costly manual process
that their model removes*. Our idea files should each name the process being replaced.
→ [[uzbek-document-ocr]] (re-typing documents) · [[sign-language-translator]] (hiring an interpreter)

Цель файла: собрать конкретные кейсы международных AI/FinTech хакатонов, чтобы использовать их как материал для подготовки к будущим global hackathons.

Категория / команда

Проблема: сотрудники вручную просматривают множество источников регуляторной информации, что медленно и чревато задержками.Решение: AI централизованно находит регуляторные обновления, помогает определить изменения и упростить соблюдение требований.Приз: $100,000.
Silver — Cyber ThirdEye
Проблема: сложно управлять киберрисками множества third-party vendors.Решение: единый dashboard с risk scores для поставщиков и визуализацией рисков.Приз: $75,000.

Проблема: ошибки и ручная работа при trade settlement, риск settlement failures и необходимость управления ликвидностью.Решение: AI проверяет сделки, обнаруживает ошибки, оптимизирует ликвидность, прогнозирует потребности и использует AI agent для проверки цепочек событий.Приз: $40,000.

Проблема: пользователям трудно планировать бюджет, инвестиции и покупки.Решение: AI personal finance tool с predictive analytics.Приз: €2,500.

Онлайн global hackathon: 1,186 участников. Phase I имел $200,000 общего prize pool; 6 finalists получили по $10,000 и приглашение в Phase II, где был отдельный prize pool $140,000.

6. Что общего у сильных решений
Конкретная дорогая проблема финансовой компании, а не абстрактный «AI chatbot».
AI встроен непосредственно в процесс: detection, compliance, settlement, risk scoring, forecasting или automation.
Есть понятный пользователь и buyer: банк, compliance team, trader, CISO, financial institution.
Результат можно измерить: меньше ручной работы, меньше ошибок, быстрее processing, лучше risk detection.
За 24–48 часов можно показать рабочий prototype/demo, даже если production-система сложнее.
